# Arcade art

Created 2026-09-28 with the built-in imagegen tool (stylized-concept).
These are original generated game assets, not screenshots of the reference boards.

- `shark-background.png`: sunlit underwater ancient ruins, clear center, coral at edges, no UI/text/creatures.
- `shark-sprites.png`: transparent 2x2 atlas: yellow submarine facing right, blue shark facing left, cyan mako, purple eel. Large shark variant reuses the blue shark at a larger scale.
- `race-background.png`: sunny empty cartoon kart circuit, palms, grandstands, checkered arch; no cars/text/UI.
- `race-sprites.png`: transparent 2x2 atlas of rear-view karts, blue player/red/green/purple opponents.
- `robot-background.png`: futuristic desert training base at sunset with a clear central runway.
- `robot-sprites.png`: transparent 2x2 atlas of blue, yellow, red and purple training robots.
- `monkey-background.png`: sunny jungle canopy with a clear central fall zone.
- `monkey-sprites.png`: transparent 2x2 atlas of banana, mango, dragon fruit and rambutan.
- `monkey-mascot.png`: transparent little monkey explorer character used as the player mascot.

- `postcard_rooftop_background.png`: rooftop background banner with houses, chimneys, antennas, and skyline under bright daylight.
- `cat_courier_idle.png`: cute courier cat in idle standing pose wearing mailbag.
- `cat_courier_run_01.png`: running animation frame 1 with mailbag.
- `cat_courier_run_02.png`: running animation frame 2 with mailbag.
- `cat_courier_jump.png`: courier cat leaping high in the air with mailbag.
- `cat_courier_land.png`: courier cat touching down/landing on the rooftop.
- `obstacle_parcel_stack.png`: stacked mail parcels obstacle on rooftop.
- `obstacle_puddle.png`: rooftop puddle obstacle.
- `obstacle_rooftop_vent.png`: metallic rooftop ventilation duct obstacle.
- `reward_envelope.png`: glowing gold-sealed reward letter envelope that floats up when jumping over obstacles.

Prompt constraints: polished child-friendly 3D animation style; separate sprites in equal quadrants; no text, UI, logos or watermarks. Backgrounds and sprites are rendered separately by ArcadeView. Ocean depth uses four increasingly deep lighting levels of the same scene.
